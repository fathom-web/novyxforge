BEHIND THE FORGE / THE PLANNING WORLD

How can a complex planning event become a sequence of clear decisions?

SAFe Agile Forge combines role-based ceremony preparation with a PI Event Command Center. Its browser-based approach brings dates, responsibilities, readiness and event artifacts into a workspace that can be used without a hosted planning backend.

01

Start with your responsibility

The documented role paths cover RTE, Product Manager, Product Owner and Scrum Master. Select ceremonies, enter iteration dates and preview a proposed calendar.

02

Prepare the whole event

The PI planner documents ART and team context, a two-day program, capacity and overlap checks, objectives, dependencies, ROAM risks and confidence records.

03

Make the plan portable

The repository describes calendar, PDF, XLSX and JSON outputs. Calendar files require an explicit import; they do not automatically send invitations.

The workflow, under the surface.

  1. Role / ART context
  2. Browser-local plan
  3. Readiness + checks
  4. ICS / PDF / XLSX

A simplified reading of the documented workflow.

01 / ENGINEERING NOTELocal-first planning has a boundary

Event state is stored per browser in localStorage, with JSON backup/import. The static architecture avoids server setup but does not provide synchronized multi-organizer editing or centralized RSVP collection.

02 / ENGINEERING NOTEExports are deliberate handoffs

Offline artifacts let an organizer review and share a plan. The RSVP workflow requires the participant to send an email or export a CSV; displaying a form does not mean the response was delivered.

03 / ENGINEERING NOTEA planning aid needs human judgment

Readiness markers, business value and confidence controls record proposed planning information. They are not organizational signoff. The source documentation leaves full browser and cross-device acceptance open.
