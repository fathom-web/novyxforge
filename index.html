01 / What is NOVYX?

Curiosity is the spark.
Creation is the point.

An independent AI and technology lab where ideas are explored, tested and turned into digital experiences. From practical tools to creative experiments, NOVYX is built around one simple belief: the best ideas deserve to become real.

It starts with curiosity. The rest is experimentation, design, code and a willingness to discover something unexpected.
